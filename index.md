## About Me
I’m an incoming Assistant Professor of Computer Science at NYU, starting in Fall 2027. I’m currently a Senior Software Architect at NVIDIA working on AI Systems and Networking. I received my Ph.D. from MIT CSAIL, where I worked in the [Network and Mobile Systems (NMS)](https://nms-r1.csail.mit.edu/) research group with Professor [Manya Ghobadi](https://people.csail.mit.edu/ghobadi/). Before MIT, I received my B.S. in computer science (honors), advised by Professor [Alex C. Snoeren](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~snoeren/), and my B.S. in physics from UC San Diego.

My research interests span all layers of networked AI systems, from hardware interconnects to application-specific optimizations. Examples from my past work include network architectures for ML training, transport protocols, cluster scheduling, and checkpointing.
